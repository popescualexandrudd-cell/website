## 2. What data we process, why and on what legal basis
| Data | Purpose | Basis (GDPR, Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone, date of birth, language | Your account, bookings, age check (the league is 18+) | performance of contract (b) |

We never sell your data or use it for personalised advertising.

## 3. What is public
On the website, the club screens and the kiosks only these appear: **first name, last name, level (rank and numeric level), points and ranking position**. Detailed statistics are visible only to you, in your account.

## 4. Who receives the data
Only the providers who help us operate, under contract and only on our instructions:
- server hosting;
- the email provider;
- the fiscal cash register;
- once enabled: the online payment processor, Apple Wallet and Google Wallet (only if you add your card there) and the AI provider.

We also share data with authorities when the law requires it. The data stays in the European Union. Any transfer outside it happens only with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

## 5. How long we keep data
- **Your account:** while it is active. When it is deleted, your personal data is erased. In other players' league history you remain shown as "Retired player", so that their results stay correct.
- **Fiscal and accounting documents:** as long as the law requires. **[Exact periods — DE_CONFIRMAT with the accountant]**.
- **Waitlist:** 7 days if you do not confirm; otherwise until 12 months after opening, or until you unsubscribe.
- **Security and audit logs:** **[DE_CONFIRMAT]**.

## 6. Your rights
You can ask at any time:
- to access your data;
- to have it corrected;
- to have it erased;
- to restrict its processing;
- for data portability (you can also download a complete export from your account once the account area launches).

You can object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw any consent at any time. We reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro).

## 7. Minors
Accounts for people under 14 are created by a parent. The league is for adults only. For people under 16 we do not use consent-based processing (such as marketing messages) without a parent's consent.

## 8. Automated decisions
League rating and rank are calculated automatically, under public rules, from results confirmed by all players. They have no legal effect on you. You can dispute any result with the club staff.

## 9. Security
Your data is protected by:
- encrypted connections;
- two-step authentication for staff;
- logs that cannot be altered;
- encrypted backups;
- access on a strict need-to-know basis.
