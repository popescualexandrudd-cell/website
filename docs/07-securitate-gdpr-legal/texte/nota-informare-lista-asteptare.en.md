# Privacy notice for the Jungle Padel waitlist

## Who processes the data
The data controller is **[company name — DE_CONFIRMAT]**, registered at **[address — DE_CONFIRMAT]**, tax ID **[CUI — DE_CONFIRMAT]**. You can write to us about your data at **[data protection email — DE_CONFIRMAT]**.

## What data we process
- your name and email address;
- your phone number, only if you give it to us;
- what you are interested in (padel, league, tennis, pilates, events, café, corporate packages) and your preferred language;
- proof of consent: date and time, the version of this notice, the IP address and browser type at the time you signed up.

## Why
To let you know about the opening of the Jungle Padel club, the Jungle league, the launch events and offers. We send you nothing else and we never sell your data.

## Legal basis
Your **consent** (Article 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with one click, from the unsubscribe link in any email. Withdrawal does not affect anything lawfully done before it.

## Who has access
Only the Jungle Padel team handling communication and the providers who help us technically, under contract and only on our instructions: server hosting and the email provider. The data stays in the European Union; if a provider ever transferred data outside it, we would do so only with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

## How long we keep it
- if you do not confirm your email within 7 days, your data is deleted automatically;
- after confirmation, we keep it for up to 12 months after the club opens, or until you unsubscribe, if earlier;
- when you unsubscribe, we delete your name, email, phone and interests; we keep only a cryptographic fingerprint of the address, as proof that you withdrew consent.

## Age
You can join the list if you are at least **16 years old**.

## Your rights
You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to us at the address above. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.

## Automated decisions
We make no automated decisions and no profiling based on this data.
