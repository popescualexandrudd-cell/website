# Consent to personal data processing in the Jungle Padel League

Please read carefully before you tick the box. Without this consent you cannot join the league. You can use every other service of the club (bookings, training, Pilates, the café) without it.

## Who processes the data
**[Company name — DE_CONFIRMAT]**, registration code **[CUI — DE_CONFIRMAT]**, registered office **[address — DE_CONFIRMAT]**. Write to us about your data at **[data protection email — DE_CONFIRMAT]**.

## Who can join
The league is for people aged 18 or over only. We check your age from the date of birth in your account.

## What data we process for the league
- your matches: date, court, partners and opponents, the score confirmed at the League Kiosk;
- the results calculated from them: rating (hidden), level, rank, league points (LP), place in the standings, badges and rewards;
- this consent: the version of the text, the date and time, the kiosk where you signed and the language shown.

## What becomes public
On the website, on the club's screens and at the kiosks, the following appears about you:
- your first and last name;
- your level (rank and numeric level);
- your league points (LP) and your place in the standings;
- **your league matches**: date, time, court, partners and opponents, the score and the league points (LP) won or lost, gathered in a **match history** on your public page;
- in tournaments: the **court and time** of your matches and their results;
- the "**Match of the day**", if you are chosen, with its court and time.

Nothing else becomes public: not your date of birth, phone number, email, badges or detailed statistics. You see those only in your own account. If you withdraw your consent or delete your account, you appear as "Retired player" in the others' past results.

## Why, and on what legal basis
- Running the league (scheduling, validating scores, calculating points and standings): our contract with you (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)).
- The public display described above: **your consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box at the League Kiosk.

## How long we keep the data
- For as long as you play in the league and, after that, for as long as the standings of the seasons you played in exist.
- If you withdraw your consent or delete your account, your name no longer appears. In other players' match history you remain as "Retired player", so that their results and points stay correct.
- The proof of this consent (version, date, kiosk) is kept for as long as the law requires, so that we can show you gave it.

## Withdrawing your consent
You can withdraw your consent at any time, from your account. Withdrawal does not affect what was lawfully published before it. After withdrawing, you can no longer play league matches. You can come back at any time by signing again at the League Kiosk.

## Your rights
You have the right to:
- access your data and get a copy (download the full export from your account);
- have it corrected;
- have it erased;
- restrict its processing;
- object to its processing;
- withdraw your consent.

We answer within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.

## Automated decisions
Rating, level and rank are calculated automatically, following public rules (the league rules), only from scores confirmed by all players at the League Kiosk. They have no legal effect on you. You can contest any result with the club's staff.
