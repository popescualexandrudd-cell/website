# Cookie policy

## 1. What we use
| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `jp_consent` | necessary | remembers your cookie choice | 12 months |
| `csrftoken` | necessary | protects forms against attacks | 1 year |
| `sessionid` | necessary | keeps you signed in (once the account area launches) | up to 14 days |

**Statistics (only with your consent):** we use a self-hosted statistics tool (Umami) that sets no cookies and does not tell us who you are. Even so, we start it only after you accept statistics.

**Marketing:** we use no marketing or advertising cookies.

## 2. Third-party services
We load no maps, fonts, videos or other third-party elements that set cookies. The map link opens OpenStreetMap in a new window, only when you click it.

## 3. How to change your choice
Use the **"Cookie settings"** link in the footer of every page. You can also delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.
