# Cancellation and refund policy

These rules apply equally to padel, tennis, pilates and coaching sessions. They are also shown in the club rules.

## 1. Cancelling at least 24 hours ahead
- It is **free**.
- If the booking was already paid, the amount becomes **credit in your account**, to use for later bookings or purchases at the club. **[Cash refund on request: DE_CONFIRMAT (Q14)]**.
- If you used a membership session, the session is returned to you. You can use it until the end of your membership period.

## 2. Cancelling less than 24 hours ahead
The booking is **charged in full**.

## 3. No-shows
- If none of the expected players scans their card within **15 minutes** of the start, the booking counts as a no-show and **is charged**.
- After the third no-show, new bookings are blocked until the club staff review the situation.

## 4. Waiting list
When a slot becomes free, the next person on the waiting list gets the booking automatically and is notified. If you get a slot less than 24 hours ahead, you can cancel it for free within 2 hours of the notification.

## 5. Memberships
- Unused sessions do not carry over to the next month.
- A membership can be frozen for up to 2 weeks a year; its end date moves accordingly.

## 6. Online payments (once available)
When online card payment is available, approved refunds go back to the same card, within the payment processor's timeframe. **[Exact timeframe — DE_CONFIRMAT (Q9)]**.

## 7. Right of withdrawal
- For bookings with a fixed date and time, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply, under Art. 16(l) of Romanian Emergency Ordinance no. 34/2014 (leisure services for a specific date). The cancellation rules above apply.
- For memberships bought online, you may withdraw within 14 days of purchase. If you asked to start using the membership during that period, you pay only for the part already used.

## 8. How to ask for a refund
Write to **[email — DE_CONFIRMAT]** or ask at reception. We reply within 14 days. Every refund or credit appears in your account history.
