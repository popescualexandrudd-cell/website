# The club's rules

These rules apply to everyone who books, plays, trains or attends events at Jungle Padel. The details about money and personal data are in the Terms and conditions, the Cancellation and refund policy and the Privacy policy.

## 1. Opening hours and time bands
- The club is open **every day, from 08:00 to 23:00**.
- The price of a booking depends on its time band: **peak 17:00–22:00**, **semi-peak 08:00–12:00 and 15:00–17:00**, **off-peak 12:00–15:00 and after 22:00**. A booking across several bands is paid in proportion, per half hour.
- The current prices are shown when booking, on the site and at the kiosk.

## 2. The account and the card
- Everyone who plays, trains or joins the league has **their own account**, with a confirmed email. An own account is possible from age 14; for younger children, a parent creates it.
- Everyone gets a **card with a QR code** (in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or printed). The card is used at the entrance, on the court, in classes, in the league, to pay and at the café. A lost card is blocked from the account or at the reception and a new one is issued.

## 3. Bookings
- You book online (the site or the installed app) or at the reception, as far ahead as you like.
- The minimum length is **60 minutes**; lengths grow in steps of 30 minutes, and start times are on the half hour.
- The organiser is enough to book; **when entering the court, every player scans their card**.
- The type of booking (rental, training, official league match, challenge, tournament, lesson) can be changed until the first scan.
- Please leave the court at the end time, so the next players start on time.

## 4. Paying
- **There are no credits for padel:** the court hour is paid. Players can **split it** at the Payments Kiosk: each scans their card and pays their share; the organiser can also pay it all.
- At the launch, the Payments Kiosk takes **cash** and gives change; every payment gets a fiscal receipt. An online booking is paid at the club; what is left to pay shows in the account.

## 5. Cancelling, no-shows, the waiting list
- Cancelling **at least 24 hours ahead** costs nothing: a paid hour becomes credit in the account, and a subscription session is given back.
- Cancelling **less than 24 hours ahead** is paid.
- A **no-show** is paid. A no-show means that none of the expected players scanned their card within 15 minutes of the start.
- **At the third no-show**, online bookings are blocked until the coach or the club's manager unblocks them.
- **Waiting list:** when a place frees up, the first person on the list gets it automatically and is told. Whoever gets a place less than 24 hours ahead can cancel it for free within the first 2 hours.

## 6. Subscriptions
- Subscriptions cover **training** (lessons with a coach, group classes, Pilates Reformer); court rental is paid separately, per hour.
- Intensities: **Start (4 sessions a month), Active (8), Pro (12)** for each sport; other intensities, "on request", at the reception.
- **Start is not used at peak time (17:00–22:00).** Active and Pro can be used at any time.
- **Unused sessions do not carry over** to the next month.
- A subscription can be **frozen for up to 14 days a year**, and is extended accordingly.

## 7. Pilates Reformer and classes
The same cancellation and no-show rules as for the courts, with an automatic waiting list. Attendance is recorded by scanning the card at the entrance.

## 8. The Jungle League
- The league is for **adults (18+)**, with the **league consent signed at the League Kiosk**, in the club, and the level validated by a coach.
- **The score of a league match is entered and confirmed only at the League Kiosk**, after the booking's end time, within 30 minutes, by every player with their own card. The match counts once all have confirmed and the hour is paid.
- The site shows the name, rank, level, points (LP), place in the standings and the results of league matches.
- The full league rules are on the League page.

## 9. Behaviour at the club
- Respect for the other players, the staff and the club's equipment.
- Courts are entered with clean sports shoes suited to synthetic grass.
- **Smoking**, including e-cigarettes and heated tobacco, is forbidden throughout the club.
- **Children under 14** stay at the club only with an adult responsible for them.
- **Animals** are not allowed, except guide dogs and assistance dogs.
- Photograph or film other people only with their consent.

## 10. The changing rooms, the café and the event room
- **Changing rooms:** lockers are used only while you are at the club and emptied when you leave. Do not leave valuables unattended: the club is not responsible for them. Forgotten items are handed in at reception and kept for 30 days.
- **Café:** food and drinks brought from outside are not consumed in the café. On the courts, only water or drinks in closed containers, never glass.
- **The event room** is used only with a booking confirmed by the manager, within the confirmed time and the club's hours. It is handed back as it was received; damage is paid by whoever booked the room.

## 11. Changes
These rules may change; the version in force is always the one on this page, with its publication date.
